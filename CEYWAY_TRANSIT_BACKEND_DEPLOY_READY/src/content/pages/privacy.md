## Information we collect

When you submit a website enquiry, CEYWAY TRANSIT collects your name, email address, any telephone number you provide, and the message you send. For trip requests we may also collect intended travel date, group size, and travel interests. We record the time you agreed to be contacted.

## How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your travel enquiry, plan itineraries, and communicate with you about your request. We do not ask for passport numbers or card payment details in these forms. We do not sell your enquiry information.

## Hosting and security

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Enquiries are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. The owner-only enquiry dashboard is designed to be protected by Cloudflare Access. Turnstile is used to help prevent automated submissions. Cloudflare may process technical data to operate these services.

## WhatsApp and email

If you contact us via WhatsApp or email, those third-party providers process messages according to their respective policies.

## Retention and your requests

We keep enquiries only as long as required to handle them and comply with applicable obligations. **Business owner: set a specific retention period and deletion process before launch.** Contact **ceywaytransit@gmail.com** to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your enquiry data, subject to applicable law.
